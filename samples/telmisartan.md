## Motivation

<!-- This section was written only after the rest of the document was completed, so that it reflects the full scope of the review. -->

Telmisartan is a prescription medication for high blood pressure. It blocks a hormone that tightens blood vessels and makes the body hold on to salt, so the vessels relax and pressure falls. It draws interest from health-focused adults because a single daily dose lasts around the clock and because laboratory work suggests it also acts on a cellular switch that governs how the body handles sugar and fat.

The drug has been sold for decades and was tested in some of the largest heart-protection trials ever run, involving tens of thousands of people at high risk of heart attack and stroke. Its possible effects on metabolism and the kidneys led longevity-oriented physicians and writers to single it out among blood pressure medications, and it was later included in a major government-funded program that tests compounds for effects on lifespan in mice.

This review examines the evidence for and against telmisartan use by health- and longevity-focused adults, covering its effects on the heart and blood vessels, the kidneys and metabolism, its risks, and how it is used in practice.

These systematic reviews and meta-analyses cover telmisartan's blood pressure, metabolic, kidney and liver effects and the principal cancer-risk question.

* [Telmisartan-based monotherapy and combination regimens for blood pressure control in adults with hypertension: a systematic review, meta-analysis, and GRADE assessment.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42693416/) - Alsel et al., 2026

  Pools 24 trials (6,521 adults); telmisartan regimens lowered office and 24-hour blood pressure, with low-to-moderate GRADE (evidence-certainty rating) certainty.

* [Telmisartan Improves Insulin Resistance: A Meta-Analysis.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29557807/) - Wang et al., 2018

  Pools 21 head-to-head trials (1,679 patients) showing small insulin-resistance and fasting-glucose advantages over other drugs in the same class.

* [Effects of telmisartan on proteinuria or albuminuria: a meta-analysis of randomized trials.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22560941/) - Takagi et al., 2013

  Pools 20 randomized trials (over 25,000 patients); telmisartan reduced proteinuria or albuminuria (protein or albumin leaking into urine) against every comparator type.

* [Efficacy of Telmisartan in MASLD/NAFLD: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis of Randomized Controlled Trials.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42614039/) - Shojaei et al., 2026

  Six small trials in MASLD/NAFLD (fatty liver disease linked to metabolic dysfunction) showed no consistent improvement in liver enzymes, liver tissue findings or insulin resistance.

* [Angiotensin-receptor blockade and risk of cancer: meta-analysis of randomised controlled trials.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20542468/) - Sipahi et al., 2010

  Covers the principal risk debate: telmisartan made up 85.7% of drug-treated patients; reported a modest rise in new cancer diagnoses, later disputed.

  
## Mechanism of Action

Telmisartan is an angiotensin receptor blocker (a drug class that stops the hormone angiotensin II from acting on its receptor). It blocks the AT1 receptor (angiotensin II type 1 receptor, the hormone's main vessel-tightening docking site) with more than 3,000-fold selectivity over the AT2 receptor (a second subtype), per US Food and Drug Administration prescribing information drawn from manufacturer data ([FDA label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=c6eb489d-2946-4c14-839f-d4af761ead31)). This interrupts the RAAS (renin-angiotensin-aldosterone system, the hormone cascade that raises blood pressure and retains salt): arteries relax, aldosterone (a salt-retaining adrenal hormone) falls, and kidney filter pressure drops, reducing albumin leakage.

A second action, partial PPAR-γ activation at concentrations reached with standard dosing in cell and rat studies ([Benson 2004](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15007034/)), is proposed to explain metabolic effects unique within the class. A competing view holds the action too weak to matter clinically: a placebo-controlled trial in overweight adults without hypertension or diabetes found no insulin-sensitivity gain ([Hsueh 2010](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20883237/); Boehringer Ingelheim-sponsored), so metabolic changes may stem from angiotensin blockade instead.

Key pharmacological properties ([FDA label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=c6eb489d-2946-4c14-839f-d4af761ead31)):

* **Half-life:** about 24 hours; peak levels at 0.5–1 hour
* **Absorption:** bioavailability 42% (40 mg) to 58% (160 mg)
* **Distribution:** over 99.5% protein-bound; extensive tissue uptake (volume of distribution about 500 liters)
* **Metabolism:** conjugation to an inactive acyl glucuronide, mainly by UGT1A3 (an enzyme that attaches glucuronic acid to drugs for excretion) ([Hirvensalo 2020](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32498119/)); no metabolism by cytochrome P450 (the main family of drug-processing liver enzymes)
* **Elimination:** over 97% excreted unchanged in feces via bile

  
## Historical Context & Evolution

Telmisartan (code BIBR 277) was developed by Boehringer Ingelheim for high blood pressure and approved in late 1998 in the United States ([Drugs@FDA NDA 020850](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cder/daf/index.cfm?event=overview.process&ApplNo=020850)) and the European Union, per the European Medicines Agency ([EMA Micardis EPAR](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/medicines/human/EPAR/micardis)).

The manufacturer funded a large outcome program. In ONTARGET, telmisartan 80 mg matched the ACE inhibitor (angiotensin-converting enzyme inhibitor, a drug class that blocks production of angiotensin II) ramipril for cardiovascular events in 25,620 high-risk patients ([Yusuf 2008](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18378520/); sponsored by Boehringer Ingelheim). This led to a 2009 indication for cardiovascular risk reduction in people unable to take ACE inhibitors ([Drugs@FDA NDA 020850](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cder/daf/index.cfm?event=overview.process&ApplNo=020850)).

Health-optimization interest grew in 2004, when laboratory work showed partial PPAR-γ activation ([Benson 2004](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15007034/)), and later when mice lacking the AT1a receptor (the mouse counterpart of AT1) lived longer ([Benigni 2009](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19197138/)). Meta-analyses then reported small metabolic advantages over other drugs in the class ([Takagi 2012](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22192303/); [Wang 2018](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29557807/)).

A 2010 meta-analysis reported more new cancers with the class, driven mainly by telmisartan trials ([Sipahi 2010](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20542468/)). A larger network meta-analysis (comparing many treatments at once) found no excess for the class alone but could not exclude one with ACE inhibitor combinations ([Bangalore 2011](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21123111/)).

Recent trials found telmisartan did not improve walking in leg artery disease ([McDermott 2022](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36194220/)) or slow aneurysm (artery bulge) growth ([Golledge 2020](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32845283/)), and a mouse lifespan test combined with a statin (a cholesterol-lowering drug) was negative ([Korstanje 2026](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41843349/)). The US brand Micardis is discontinued; FDA found its 20 and 80 mg strengths not withdrawn for safety ([Drugs@FDA NDA 020850](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cder/daf/index.cfm?event=overview.process&ApplNo=020850)).

  
## Expected Benefits

### High 🟩 🟩 🟩

#### Blood pressure reduction

Telmisartan lowers blood pressure through AT1 receptor blockade, with a 24-hour duration from one daily dose. Placebo-controlled trials summarized in the FDA label (sponsored by the manufacturer) are supported by independent syntheses: a Cochrane review of the drug class, including telmisartan trials, by Heran's group ([Heran 2008](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18843650/)) and a 24-trial meta-analysis by Alsel's group ([Alsel 2026](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42693416/)). For people starting from high-normal readings the fall is smaller, as a meta-analysis of baseline effects shows ([Wang 2025](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40885583/)).

**Magnitude:** At 80 mg, systolic/diastolic pressure fell about 12–13/7–8 mm Hg more than placebo ([FDA label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=c6eb489d-2946-4c14-839f-d4af761ead31)); across 24 trials, office systolic pressure fell 6.39 mm Hg more than comparators (95% CI, the range likely to contain the true value, −7.86 to −4.93) ([Alsel 2026](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42693416/)).

#### Reduction of urinary albumin leakage

Albuminuria (albumin protein leaking into urine, an early sign of kidney and blood vessel damage) falls with telmisartan through lower pressure in the kidney's filters. Takagi's group pooled 20 randomized trials ([Takagi 2013](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22560941/)), Bakris's group showed a larger fall than with losartan in diabetic kidney disease ([Bakris 2008](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18496508/); Boehringer Ingelheim-sponsored), and Mann's group found less albumin rise than with ramipril ([Mann 2008](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18707986/); ONTARGET analysis, Boehringer Ingelheim-funded). Hard kidney outcomes were similar to ACE inhibitors, and the benefit applies mainly to people who already leak albumin.

**Magnitude:** Percent change in urinary protein or albumin was 24.36 percentage points lower than with controls (95% CI −32.85 to −15.87) across 20 trials ([Takagi 2013](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22560941/)).

### Medium 🟩 🟩

#### Fewer heart attacks, strokes and cardiovascular deaths ⚠️ Conflicted

In ONTARGET, telmisartan matched ramipril, a drug with proven event reduction ([Yusuf 2008](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18378520/)). Against placebo, TRANSCEND missed its primary outcome but reduced cardiovascular death, heart attack or stroke at borderline significance ([Yusuf 2008](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18757085/)), and PRoFESS found no reduction in recurrent stroke ([Yusuf 2008](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18753639/)). All three came from one research group with manufacturer funding. A low-dose telmisartan triple combination cut recurrent stroke after brain hemorrhage ([Anderson 2026](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42019018/)), indirect evidence because two other drugs were given. Net reading: protection resembles an ACE inhibitor, with a modest, uncertain margin over placebo.

**Magnitude:** Cardiovascular death, heart attack or stroke occurred in 13.0% on telmisartan versus 14.8% on placebo (HR, hazard ratio, the relative event rate over time, 0.87; 95% CI 0.76–1.00) ([Yusuf 2008](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18757085/)).

#### Insulin sensitivity and blood sugar control ⚠️ Conflicted

Meta-analyses ([Wang 2018](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29557807/); [Takagi 2013](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23523138/)) found small gains in insulin resistance, fasting glucose and HbA1c (glycated hemoglobin, a three-month blood sugar average) in hypertension or metabolic syndrome (clustered high blood pressure, abdominal fat, high blood sugar and abnormal blood fats). Contrary evidence: a placebo-controlled trial in overweight adults with normal blood pressure found none ([Hsueh 2010](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20883237/)), and new-onset diabetes was not significantly reduced after stroke ([Yusuf 2008](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18753639/)). Net reading: a small blood sugar gain appears with high blood pressure and insulin resistance, not with normal blood pressure.

**Magnitude:** HbA1c change was lower than with control therapy by a standardized mean difference (SMD, an effect size in standard-deviation units) of −0.26 (95% CI −0.44 to −0.08) in metabolic syndrome ([Takagi 2013](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23523138/)).

#### Fewer atrial fibrillation recurrences ⚠️ Conflicted

Atrial fibrillation (an irregular, rapid heart rhythm that raises stroke risk) recurred less often with telmisartan than with other antihypertensives (blood pressure-lowering drugs) in a meta-analysis of four trials ([Pan 2014](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24645740/)) and than with carvedilol in Galzerano's trial ([Galzerano 2012](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22546623/)). Contrary evidence: in 149 patients, overall recurrence matched nifedipine (55.4% vs 58.7%), though fewer progressed to persistent fibrillation ([Du 2013](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23438932/)). Net reading: a possible benefit in hypertensive people with intermittent fibrillation, resting on small trials.

**Magnitude:** HR 0.54 (95% CI 0.34–0.86) for recurrence versus other antihypertensive drugs in 1,050 hypertensive patients ([Pan 2014](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24645740/)).

### Low 🟩

#### Protection against cognitive decline ⚠️ Conflicted

Claims data linked higher telmisartan exposure with less Alzheimer's disease in African Americans only ([Zhang 2023](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36331056/)). Randomized trials found no cognitive benefit versus placebo ([Anderson 2011](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20980201/); [Diener 2008](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18757238/); both Boehringer Ingelheim-funded trial analyses). Net reading: the stronger randomized evidence shows no protection.

**Magnitude:** Cognitive impairment occurred in 9% on telmisartan and 9% on placebo (OR, odds ratio, the relative odds of the outcome between groups, 0.97; 95% CI 0.81–1.17) ([Anderson 2011](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20980201/)).

#### Improvement of fatty liver disease ⚠️ Conflicted

Small early trials, summarized in a brief review ([Borém 2018](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29636553/)), suggested benefit in MASLD (metabolic dysfunction-associated steatotic liver disease, liver fat buildup linked to metabolic problems). A six-trial meta-analysis found no consistent enzyme or liver tissue gain ([Shojaei 2026](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42614039/)). Net reading: no reliable liver benefit.

**Magnitude:** Fibrosis stage changed by −0.28 versus controls (95% CI −0.57 to 0.01), not significant ([Shojaei 2026](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42614039/)).

#### Better walking in leg artery disease ⚠️ Conflicted

A small single-blind trial reported longer walking distance in peripheral artery disease (narrowed leg arteries causing pain on walking) ([Zankl 2010](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20614124/)). The larger double-blind TELEX trial found no improvement ([McDermott 2022](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36194220/)). Net reading: the better trial shows no benefit.

**Magnitude:** Six-minute walk distance changed by −16.8 m versus placebo (95% CI −35.9 to 2.2) ([McDermott 2022](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36194220/)).

#### Slower growth of small aortic aneurysms ⚠️ Conflicted

Preclinical studies, summarized in a review ([Rowbotham 2018](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29284388/)), suggested telmisartan slows abdominal aortic aneurysm (bulging of the main abdominal artery) growth. The placebo-controlled TEDY trial found no effect but was underpowered (too small to detect a modest effect) ([Golledge 2020](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32845283/)). Net reading: no human benefit shown.

**Magnitude:** Growth differed by −0.11 mm per year versus placebo (95% CI −0.60 to 0.38) ([Golledge 2020](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32845283/)).

### Speculative 🟨

#### Lifespan extension

Mice lacking the AT1a receptor lived longer ([Benigni 2009](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19197138/)), but atorvastatin plus telmisartan did not extend lifespan in genetically diverse mice ([Korstanje 2026](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41843349/)). No human lifespan data exist.

#### Lower inflammatory markers

Meta-analyses report lower CRP (C-reactive protein, a blood marker of inflammation) and IL-6 (interleukin-6, an inflammatory signaling protein) with telmisartan ([Takagi 2012](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22353440/); [Takagi 2013](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23235712/)). These markers are unvalidated as outcome surrogates.

#### Less visceral fat, lower triglycerides and higher adiponectin

Meta-analyses report less visceral (abdominal organ) fat than controls ([Choi 2016](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27010868/)) and, versus other drugs in the class, lower triglycerides ([Takagi 2012](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22386702/)) and higher adiponectin ([Takagi 2012](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22192290/)). None has been tied to fewer clinical events.

#### Better vessel function and less heart muscle thickening

Meta-analyses report improved FMD (flow-mediated dilation, an artery-relaxation ultrasound test) ([Takagi 2014](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24718299/)), lower arm-to-ankle arterial stiffness in a telmisartan subgroup ([Peng 2015](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25854987/)) and left ventricular mass regression ([Takagi 2013](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23041001/)). None is tied to events.

#### Stronger muscle response to exercise

In 13 TELEX participants who exercised, telmisartan increased muscle fiber size and lowered myostatin (a protein that limits muscle growth) versus placebo ([Kosmac 2025](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40837832/)). The basis is biopsy markers only.

  
## Benefit-Modifying Factors

* **Genetic variants:** The UGT1A3\*2 variant was associated with 57–72% lower telmisartan exposure, and an exposure-based genotype score tracked diastolic pressure response ([Hirvensalo 2020](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32498119/)), which may blunt response at standard doses.
* **Baseline blood pressure:** Blood pressure falls less from lower starting values, by about 1.3 mm Hg per 10 mm Hg lower baseline for single drugs in a meta-analysis ([Wang 2025](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40885583/)), so people with high-normal readings gain less.
* **Baseline albuminuria:** Kidney benefit concentrates in people who already leak albumin; in those without, a post hoc analysis (one not planned in advance) suggested harm rather than benefit ([Tobe 2011](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21357827/); Boehringer Ingelheim-funded).
* **Baseline insulin resistance:** Metabolic gains appear in hypertensive or insulin-resistant patients in a meta-analysis ([Wang 2018](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29557807/)) but not in overweight adults with normal blood pressure ([Hsueh 2010](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20883237/)).
* **Sex:** Women reach plasma levels 2–3 times higher than men, without a larger blood pressure response ([FDA label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=c6eb489d-2946-4c14-839f-d4af761ead31)); no sex difference in outcome benefit has been reported.
* **Ancestry:** Blood pressure response is smaller in Black patients ([FDA label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=c6eb489d-2946-4c14-839f-d4af761ead31)), while the observational dementia association appeared only in African Americans ([Zhang 2023](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36331056/)).
* **Pre-existing conditions:** Clearest benefits appear with hypertension, diabetic kidney disease and established vascular disease; trials in leg artery disease ([McDermott 2022](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36194220/)), aneurysm ([Golledge 2020](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32845283/)) and fatty liver ([Shojaei 2026](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42614039/)) were null.
* **Age:** Pharmacokinetics (how the body absorbs, distributes and clears the drug) do not differ in older adults, and ONTARGET enrolled 15% aged 75 or older with no overall efficacy difference ([FDA label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=c6eb489d-2946-4c14-839f-d4af761ead31)).

  
## Potential Risks & Side Effects

### High 🟥 🟥 🟥

#### Low blood pressure, dizziness and fainting

Symptomatic hypotension (blood pressure low enough to cause lightheadedness or fainting) is the most consistent harm, most likely when salt or fluid is depleted. Yusuf's group reported more hypotensive symptoms than with ramipril ([Yusuf 2008](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18378520/)) and more hypotension-related discontinuation than placebo ([Yusuf 2008](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18757085/)). Golledge's independent group found hypotensive symptoms doubled in older adults ([Golledge 2020](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32845283/)). The label notes treatment usually continues once pressure stabilizes ([FDA label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=c6eb489d-2946-4c14-839f-d4af761ead31)).

**Magnitude:** Hypotensive symptoms affected 26% on telmisartan 40 mg versus 13% on placebo in adults with a mean age of 73.5 years ([Golledge 2020](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32845283/)); permanent discontinuation for hypotension was 0.98% versus 0.54% on placebo ([Yusuf 2008](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18757085/)).

### Medium 🟥 🟥

#### Decline in kidney function ⚠️ Conflicted

Lower filter pressure can reduce eGFR (estimated glomerular filtration rate, a kidney filtering measure). In ONTARGET, eGFR fell more than with ramipril ([Mann 2008](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18707986/)), and a post hoc TRANSCEND analysis found more dialysis or doubling of creatinine (a muscle waste product used to gauge kidney function) versus placebo in people without albuminuria ([Tobe 2011](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21357827/)). Contrary evidence: five-year filtration decline was not inferior to enalapril ([Barnett 2004](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15516696/); Boehringer Ingelheim-sponsored; confidence interval later corrected, conclusion unchanged). Net reading: a real risk of functional decline, most relevant to people without albuminuria.

**Magnitude:** Dialysis or creatinine doubling occurred at 0.37 versus 0.16 events per 100 patient-years on placebo in people without albuminuria (HR 2.35; 95% CI 1.33–4.15; post hoc) ([Tobe 2011](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21357827/)).

#### Hyperkalemia

Hyperkalemia (high blood potassium, which can disturb heart rhythm) arises because lower aldosterone reduces potassium excretion. The label warns of it, especially with kidney impairment, heart failure or potassium supplements ([FDA label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=c6eb489d-2946-4c14-839f-d4af761ead31)). In ONTARGET, rates matched ramipril and were higher when both drugs were combined ([Yusuf 2008](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18378520/)).

**Magnitude:** Potassium above 5.5 mmol/L occurred in 287 of 8,542 patients on telmisartan versus 283 of 8,576 on ramipril and 480 of 8,502 on the combination ([Yusuf 2008](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18378520/)).

#### Back pain, upper airway infections and diarrhea

Placebo-controlled hypertension trials of 20–160 mg, pooled in the label, found a few mild complaints more often on telmisartan: upper respiratory tract infection, back pain, sinusitis (sinus inflammation), diarrhea and pharyngitis (throat inflammation). Overall adverse events and discontinuations were not higher ([FDA label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=c6eb489d-2946-4c14-839f-d4af761ead31)). These data come from the manufacturer's trials; an independent 24-trial meta-analysis found overall adverse events comparable to comparators ([Alsel 2026](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42693416/)).

**Magnitude:** Upper respiratory tract infection 7% versus 6% on placebo, back pain 3% versus 1%, sinusitis 3% versus 2%, diarrhea 3% versus 2% and pharyngitis 1% versus 0%; discontinuation for adverse events 2.8% versus 6.1% on placebo ([FDA label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=c6eb489d-2946-4c14-839f-d4af761ead31)).

#### Leg pain on walking and skin ulcers

Intermittent claudication (leg pain on walking caused by narrowed leg arteries) and skin ulcers were the only serious adverse events at least 1 percentage point more common than placebo in TRANSCEND, per the label ([FDA label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=c6eb489d-2946-4c14-839f-d4af761ead31)). TRANSCEND was a single manufacturer-funded trial in people with vascular disease or diabetes ([Yusuf 2008](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18757085/)). No significance test is reported, and the mechanism is unknown. The concern applies mainly to people with existing artery disease.

**Magnitude:** Serious intermittent claudication 7% versus 6% on placebo and skin ulcer 3% versus 2% ([FDA label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=c6eb489d-2946-4c14-839f-d4af761ead31)).

#### Fall in hemoglobin

In placebo-controlled hypertension trials pooled in the label, all from the manufacturer, hemoglobin fell over 2 g/dL slightly more often than on placebo, with no discontinuations for anemia; anemia also appears in postmarketing reports ([FDA label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=c6eb489d-2946-4c14-839f-d4af761ead31)). An early-stopped trial of 136 kidney transplant recipients found no anemia worsening versus placebo ([Salzberg 2014](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24356394/)), but was too small to detect a difference this size. The effect is rare and small.

**Magnitude:** Hemoglobin fell by more than 2 g/dL in 0.8% on telmisartan versus 0.3% on placebo ([FDA label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=c6eb489d-2946-4c14-839f-d4af761ead31)).

### Low 🟥

#### Fetal injury

Drugs acting on the renin-angiotensin system in the second and third trimesters reduce fetal kidney function and can cause oligohydramnios (too little amniotic fluid), skull and lung underdevelopment and death, a boxed warning ([FDA label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=c6eb489d-2946-4c14-839f-d4af761ead31)). The grade reflects case reports, pooled in a systematic review ([Bullo 2012](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22753220/)), not severity.

**Magnitude:** Only 13% of 68 newborns exposed to this drug class had no complications (case reports, no control group), per a systematic review of case reports ([Bullo 2012](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22753220/)).

#### Angioedema and other allergic reactions

Angioedema (sudden swelling of lips, tongue or throat that can block breathing) and anaphylaxis (a severe whole-body allergic reaction) occur rarely; fatal angioedema and drug-induced skin rashes appear in postmarketing reports ([FDA label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=c6eb489d-2946-4c14-839f-d4af761ead31)). Angioedema was less frequent than with ramipril ([Yusuf 2008](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18378520/)).

**Magnitude:** Angioedema 0.1% on telmisartan versus 0.3% on ramipril; no placebo comparison is available, and no anaphylaxis or skin rash rate is reported ([Yusuf 2008](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18378520/)).

#### New cancer diagnoses ⚠️ Conflicted

A meta-analysis dominated by telmisartan trials reported more new cancers ([Sipahi 2010](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20542468/)). A larger network meta-analysis found no excess with the class alone, but a possible excess with ACE inhibitor combinations ([Bangalore 2011](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21123111/)). Net reading: no excess for the drug class used alone.

**Magnitude:** New cancer 7.2% versus 6.0% in controls (RR, risk ratio, the event risk in one group relative to the other, 1.08; 95% CI 1.01–1.15) ([Sipahi 2010](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20542468/)); cancer in 2.04% on the class versus 2.02% on placebo (odds ratio 1.01; 95% CI 0.93–1.09) ([Bangalore 2011](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21123111/)).

#### Sepsis ⚠️ Conflicted

Sepsis (a life-threatening body-wide reaction to infection) was flagged in two large randomized trials, per a case-control study of 550,436 hypertensive adults that this signal prompted; it found no excess sepsis with the drug class, including telmisartan ([Dial 2014](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24803383/)). Net reading: routine-use data do not confirm the signal.

**Magnitude:** Relative risk 1.09 (95% CI 0.83–1.43) for hospitalization with sepsis on drugs of the class, including telmisartan, versus users of other blood pressure-lowering drug classes ([Dial 2014](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24803383/)); the source reports no rates for each group.

#### Liver injury

Abnormal liver function appears in postmarketing reports ([FDA label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=c6eb489d-2946-4c14-839f-d4af761ead31)), and a case of severe drug-induced liver injury followed co-administration with acipimox ([Liu 2025](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40898477/)). Biliary excretion makes liver disease a risk amplifier.

**Magnitude:** Not quantified in available studies. Only spontaneous postmarketing reports and single case reports describe it.

#### Muscle pain and rhabdomyolysis

Myalgia (muscle pain), raised CPK (creatine phosphokinase, a muscle enzyme) and rare rhabdomyolysis (rapid muscle breakdown) appear in postmarketing reports ([FDA label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=c6eb489d-2946-4c14-839f-d4af761ead31)). A report of two patients describes telmisartan-induced muscle damage ([Kara 2024](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38573089/)); one rhabdomyolysis case occurred with rosuvastatin ([Gallelli 2009](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19354002/)), whose levels telmisartan raises ([Hu 2016](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27651239/)). Causality is unproven.

**Magnitude:** Not quantified in available studies. Only postmarketing reports and case reports exist, without a denominator or comparison group.

#### Low blood sodium

Hyponatremia (low blood sodium, which can cause confusion, unsteadiness and falls) appears in postmarketing reports for telmisartan ([FDA label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=c6eb489d-2946-4c14-839f-d4af761ead31)). A case report describes it, with high potassium, on telmisartan plus hydrochlorothiazide ([Cakir 2010](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20486869/)), indirect evidence because a diuretic (a drug that increases urine output) was co-administered. Causality is unproven.

**Magnitude:** Not quantified in available studies. Only spontaneous postmarketing reports and a single case report describe it.

#### Low blood sugar in people with diabetes

Hypoglycemia (low blood sugar) in people with diabetes appears in postmarketing reports for telmisartan ([FDA label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=c6eb489d-2946-4c14-839f-d4af761ead31)). A modest fasting glucose fall versus other drugs in the class ([Wang 2018](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29557807/)) is one proposed link, most relevant alongside glucose-lowering drugs. Causality is unproven.

**Magnitude:** Not quantified in available studies. Only spontaneous postmarketing reports describe it, without a denominator or comparison group.

#### Raised uric acid

Raised serum uric acid (a waste product whose buildup can trigger gout) appears in postmarketing reports for telmisartan ([FDA label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=c6eb489d-2946-4c14-839f-d4af761ead31)). In one randomized comparison, uric acid rose with telmisartan plus hydrochlorothiazide but not with losartan plus hydrochlorothiazide ([Hamada 2014](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23865441/)), indirect evidence because a diuretic was co-administered. Causality is unproven.

**Magnitude:** Over one year, serum uric acid rose significantly within the telmisartan plus hydrochlorothiazide group (22 patients) and did not change within the losartan plus hydrochlorothiazide group (37 patients), not compared between groups ([Hamada 2014](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23865441/)); the source abstract reports no group figures, and postmarketing reports give no rate or comparison group.

#### Low platelet count and raised eosinophils

Thrombocytopenia (low blood platelet count) and eosinophilia (raised allergy-related white blood cells) appear in postmarketing reports for telmisartan ([FDA label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=c6eb489d-2946-4c14-839f-d4af761ead31)). Immune thrombocytopenia has been reported with losartan, another drug in the class ([Ada 2002](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12379089/)), indirect evidence because a different compound was involved. Causality is unproven.

**Magnitude:** Not quantified in available studies. Only spontaneous postmarketing reports and a case report with a related drug describe it.

#### Erectile dysfunction ⚠️ Conflicted

Erectile dysfunction appears in postmarketing reports for telmisartan ([FDA label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=c6eb489d-2946-4c14-839f-d4af761ead31)). Contrary evidence: a meta-analysis of randomized trials found no significant change in erectile function with losartan or telmisartan versus control ([Ismail 2019](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31795911/)). Net reading: controlled data show no excess risk.

**Magnitude:** Erectile function scores did not differ significantly from control (mean difference 1.36 points, 203 men on losartan or telmisartan versus 232 controls; 95% CI −0.97 to 3.69 per the full text, which the abstract misprints as −0.97 to −3.69) ([Ismail 2019](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31795911/)); postmarketing reports give no rate or comparison group.

### Speculative 🟨

No risk is graded Speculative: every concern identified for telmisartan rests on human trial, label or case-report data, and none rests only on in-vitro or animal findings.

  
## Risk-Modifying Factors

* **Genetic variants:** UGT1A3\*2 lowers exposure by 57–72% ([Hirvensalo 2020](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32498119/)); non-carriers reach higher levels, though whether this raises hypotension risk is untested.
* **Baseline potassium and kidney function:** In chronic kidney disease, potassium above 5.0 mmol/L argues against starting, per the National Institute for Health and Care Excellence, a government body without members ([NICE NG203](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng203/chapter/Recommendations)); normal albumin excretion was linked post hoc to excess kidney events ([Tobe 2011](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21357827/)).
* **Sex:** Women have 2–3-fold higher plasma levels without more orthostatic hypotension (a fall in pressure on standing) ([FDA label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=c6eb489d-2946-4c14-839f-d4af761ead31)); women who could become pregnant face the fetal-injury risk.
* **Liver and bile disease:** Reduced biliary clearance raises exposure, with bioavailability approaching 100% in hepatic insufficiency (reduced liver function) ([FDA label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=c6eb489d-2946-4c14-839f-d4af761ead31)).
* **Kidney artery narrowing, heart failure and fluid loss:** Renal artery stenosis (narrowed kidney arteries), severe heart failure, diuretic use and dehydration increase the risk of kidney failure and hypotension ([FDA label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=c6eb489d-2946-4c14-839f-d4af761ead31)).
* **Diabetes:** Combining with aliskiren is contraindicated in diabetes, and hypoglycemia (low blood sugar) has been reported in diabetic patients ([FDA label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=c6eb489d-2946-4c14-839f-d4af761ead31)).
* **Older age:** In adults averaging 73.5 years, hypotensive symptoms doubled versus placebo ([Golledge 2020](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32845283/)); the label states that greater sensitivity in some older individuals cannot be ruled out ([FDA label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=c6eb489d-2946-4c14-839f-d4af761ead31)).

  
## Key Interactions & Contraindications

<!-- Search statement: On 2026-10-26 I searched PubMed (pubmed_search_articles) for telmisartan[ti] AND (pharmacokinetic interaction OR drug interaction OR "drug-drug interaction") AND humans (68 results) and reviewed human interaction studies with warfarin, acetaminophen, ibuprofen, rosuvastatin, metformin, mycophenolate mofetil, nisoldipine, amlodipine, hydrochlorothiazide and others, together with the drug-interaction and contraindication sections of the FDA label, the EMA EPAR summary and the NICE NG203 guideline. -->

**Documented interactions:**

* **ACE inhibitors (ramipril, lisinopril, enalapril):** Adding telmisartan raised hypotension, fainting, kidney dysfunction and hyperkalemia without extra benefit ([Yusuf 2008](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18378520/)) and raises ramipril peak levels 2.3-fold ([FDA label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=c6eb489d-2946-4c14-839f-d4af761ead31)). Avoid. Mitigation: one drug class only.
* **Aliskiren (a direct renin inhibitor, which blocks the first step of the hormone cascade):** Risk of hypotension, hyperkalemia and kidney injury. Absolute contraindication in diabetes; avoid when eGFR is below 60 mL/min ([FDA label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=c6eb489d-2946-4c14-839f-d4af761ead31)).
* **Potassium-raising products (potassium chloride supplements, potassium-containing salt substitutes, spironolactone, amiloride, eplerenone):** Hyperkalemia, as these potassium-sparing diuretics (drugs that increase urine output while retaining potassium) and supplements add to retention ([FDA label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=c6eb489d-2946-4c14-839f-d4af761ead31)). Mitigation: potassium check within 1–2 weeks.
* **NSAIDs (non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drugs, over-the-counter pain relievers such as ibuprofen and naproxen) and COX-2 inhibitors (drugs blocking the cyclooxygenase-2 enzyme, such as celecoxib):** Possible acute kidney failure in older, dehydrated or kidney-impaired people, and blunted blood pressure lowering ([FDA label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=c6eb489d-2946-4c14-839f-d4af761ead31)). Mitigation: kidney monitoring, short courses.
* **Lithium:** Reversible rises in lithium levels and toxicity ([FDA label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=c6eb489d-2946-4c14-839f-d4af761ead31); [Ma 2012](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22353332/)). Mitigation: serum lithium monitoring.
* **Digoxin:** Peak digoxin levels rose 49% and trough levels 20%, risking digoxin toxicity ([FDA label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=c6eb489d-2946-4c14-839f-d4af761ead31)). Mitigation: digoxin levels at start, dose change and stop.
* **Rosuvastatin:** Telmisartan raised rosuvastatin peak levels 71% and exposure 26% by blocking the ABCG2 transporter (a gut and liver pump that expels drugs) ([Hu 2016](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27651239/)), possibly increasing statin muscle side effects; judged not clinically significant elsewhere ([Son 2014](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24998012/)). Mitigation: reporting unexplained muscle pain.
* **Metformin:** Telmisartan lowered metformin exposure by about 17% in healthy men, with a modest effect on glucose response ([Wen 2019](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30538075/)). Mitigation: tracking glucose control.
* **Mycophenolate mofetil:** In kidney transplant recipients, telmisartan lowered exposure to mycophenolic acid (the active form of this immunosuppressant) by about 30%, possibly by inducing UGT enzymes, risking weaker immunosuppression ([Miura 2009](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20175802/)). Mitigation: periodic mycophenolic acid level checks.
* **Nisoldipine:** Co-administration raised telmisartan exposure by 132% in hypertensive patients, which could add to blood pressure lowering ([Bajcetic 2007](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17322141/)). Mitigation: blood pressure checks when the two are combined.
* **PDE5 inhibitors (erectile-dysfunction drugs such as tadalafil and sildenafil):** In a human study, tadalafil 20 mg lowered blood pressure a further 8/4 mm Hg versus placebo in people taking angiotensin receptor blockers, adding dizziness risk ([Cialis FDA label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=bcd8f8ab-81a2-4891-83db-24a0b0e25895)). Mitigation: low starting PDE5 inhibitor dose.
* **CYP2C19 substrates (omeprazole, citalopram, clopidogrel):** The label notes in-vitro inhibition of CYP2C19 (a liver enzyme that metabolizes these drugs), which could raise omeprazole or citalopram levels or reduce clopidogrel activation; no human study has tested it ([FDA label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=c6eb489d-2946-4c14-839f-d4af761ead31)).
* **Warfarin:** No interaction found; INR (a blood clotting time measure) was unchanged in a study run by the manufacturer, Boehringer Ingelheim ([Stangier 2000](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11185631/)).
* **Acetaminophen and ibuprofen pharmacokinetics:** No interaction found in drug levels in a manufacturer-run study ([Stangier 2000](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11185632/)).
* **Amlodipine, hydrochlorothiazide, glyburide, simvastatin:** No clinically significant interaction found ([FDA label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=c6eb489d-2946-4c14-839f-d4af761ead31)).

**Theoretical interactions (inferred from mechanism, not studied in humans):**

* **Blood pressure-lowering supplements (beetroot nitrate, garlic extract, hibiscus tea, magnesium, coenzyme Q10):** Additive dizziness or hypotension, inferred from additive blood pressure lowering. Mitigation: rechecking home readings after adding one.
* **Alpha-blockers (drugs that relax blood vessel and prostate muscle, such as tamsulosin and doxazosin):** Orthostatic hypotension, inferred from additive vasodilation. Mitigation: low starting dose of the alpha-blocker, slow rising.
* **UGT enzyme inducers (rifampin, carbamazepine, phenytoin):** Weaker blood pressure control, inferred from faster glucuronidation of telmisartan. Mitigation: blood pressure checks when these start or stop.
* **Licorice (glycyrrhizin-containing products):** Raised blood pressure and potassium loss, inferred from licorice's aldosterone-like effect opposing telmisartan. Mitigation: limiting regular licorice.
* **Alcohol:** Lightheadedness or fainting, inferred from additive blood pressure lowering. Mitigation: moderate intake, especially early on.

**Populations who should avoid Telmisartan:**

* Pregnant women, at any stage, and women planning pregnancy; boxed warning for fetal toxicity ([FDA label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=c6eb489d-2946-4c14-839f-d4af761ead31)); contraindicated after the first three months in the EU ([EMA Micardis EPAR](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/medicines/human/EPAR/micardis))
* Breastfeeding women ([FDA label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=c6eb489d-2946-4c14-839f-d4af761ead31))
* People with prior anaphylaxis (a severe whole-body allergic reaction) or angioedema from telmisartan ([FDA label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=c6eb489d-2946-4c14-839f-d4af761ead31))
* People with diabetes taking aliskiren, or with eGFR below 60 mL/min taking aliskiren ([FDA label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=c6eb489d-2946-4c14-839f-d4af761ead31))
* People with severe liver disease or bile-flow obstruction ([EMA Micardis EPAR](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/medicines/human/EPAR/micardis))
* People with chronic kidney disease and pretreatment potassium above 5.0 mmol/L ([NICE NG203](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng203/chapter/Recommendations))

  
## Risk Mitigation Strategies

Doses and timings below follow common practice unless cited.

* **Low starting dose:** Starting at 40 mg once daily ([FDA label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=c6eb489d-2946-4c14-839f-d4af761ead31)), with 20 mg sufficient for some patients ([EMA Micardis EPAR](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/medicines/human/EPAR/micardis)), increasing after 2–4 weeks only if needed. Mitigates symptomatic hypotension and dizziness.
* **Correct fluid and salt depletion first:** Rehydration and diuretic review before starting ([FDA label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=c6eb489d-2946-4c14-839f-d4af761ead31)). Prevents first-dose hypotension and kidney strain.
* **Early kidney and potassium labs:** Potassium and eGFR checks at baseline and 1–2 weeks after starting or each dose increase, as advised in chronic kidney disease ([NICE NG203](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng203/chapter/Recommendations)). Detects hyperkalemia and kidney function decline early.
* **Kidney stop thresholds:** Review if creatinine rises 30% or more, or eGFR falls 25% or more; stop if potassium reaches 6.0 mmol/L, per guidance for chronic kidney disease ([NICE NG203](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng203/chapter/Recommendations)). Limits kidney injury and dangerous hyperkalemia.
* **Home blood pressure log:** Seated and standing readings several times weekly during the first month; systolic readings persistently below 100 mm Hg or fainting prompt medical review. Catches excessive blood pressure lowering before falls.
* **Sick-day pause:** Doses withheld during vomiting, diarrhea, severe sweating or poor fluid intake, and resumed once eating and drinking are normal. Reduces acute kidney injury and hyperkalemia during dehydration.
* **Single-class rule:** No combination with an ACE inhibitor or, in diabetes, aliskiren ([FDA label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=c6eb489d-2946-4c14-839f-d4af761ead31)). Prevents the hypotension, hyperkalemia and kidney harms of dual blockade.
* **Pregnancy prevention:** Reliable contraception for women who could conceive, and stopping as soon as pregnancy is detected ([FDA label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=c6eb489d-2946-4c14-839f-d4af761ead31)). Prevents fetal kidney injury and death.
* **Liver dose cap:** A 40 mg daily maximum with mild or moderate liver impairment ([EMA Micardis EPAR](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/medicines/human/EPAR/micardis)). Limits drug accumulation from reduced biliary clearance.
* **Limit NSAIDs and potassium products:** Acetaminophen in place of NSAIDs for pain, and no potassium salt substitutes unless potassium is monitored. Prevents kidney injury and hyperkalemia.

  
## Therapeutic Protocol

Timing, titration steps and other parameters without a citation reflect common practice.

* **Standard hypertension dose:** 40 mg once daily, range 40–80 mg; response is dose-related from 20 mg, and doses above 80 mg add little ([FDA label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=c6eb489d-2946-4c14-839f-d4af761ead31)). The EU summary notes some patients do well on 20 mg ([EMA Micardis EPAR](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/medicines/human/EPAR/micardis)).
* **Cardiovascular risk-reduction dose:** 80 mg once daily, the regimen tested in ONTARGET and TRANSCEND ([Yusuf 2008](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18378520/)); whether lower doses reduce events is not known ([FDA label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=c6eb489d-2946-4c14-839f-d4af761ead31)).
* **Low-dose combination approach:** 20 mg combined with amlodipine 2.5 mg and indapamide 1.25 mg in one tablet ([Anderson 2026](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42019018/)); a 10–40 mg triple product is marketed as Widaplik ([Widaplik FDA label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=a30ff81d-420b-40ea-83d8-670260a93fff)).
* **Longevity-practitioner approach:** Life Extension's William Faloon describes 20, 40 or 80 mg daily for readings above 115/75 mm Hg ([Life Extension Magazine article](https://www.lifeextension.com/magazine/2015/3/best-drug-to-treat-hypertension)); Life Extension sells blood pressure supplements and laboratory testing, though not telmisartan.
* **Guideline-trial approach:** Intensive treatment to a systolic target below 120 mm Hg reduced events in high-risk adults without diabetes ([Wright 2015](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26551272/)); telmisartan is one of several drug classes able to reach it.
* **Time of day:** Morning and evening dosing of usual antihypertensives gave equal cardiovascular outcomes ([Mackenzie 2022](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36240838/)); it can be taken with or without food ([FDA label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=c6eb489d-2946-4c14-839f-d4af761ead31)).
* **Half-life:** About 24 hours, with trough levels 10–25% of peak ([FDA label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=c6eb489d-2946-4c14-839f-d4af761ead31)).
* **Single versus split dose:** One daily dose suffices given the long half-life; splitting offers no documented advantage.
* **Genetic variants:** UGT1A3\*2 carriers have much lower exposure ([Hirvensalo 2020](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32498119/)); weaker response at 40 mg may reflect genotype rather than drug failure. Routine genotyping is not used.
* **Sex:** No dose adjustment for women despite higher plasma levels ([FDA label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=c6eb489d-2946-4c14-839f-d4af761ead31)).
* **Age:** No initial adjustment for older adults, although greater sensitivity in some older individuals cannot be ruled out ([FDA label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=c6eb489d-2946-4c14-839f-d4af761ead31)).
* **Baseline biomarkers:** Baseline blood pressure, potassium, eGFR and urine albumin set the case for use and the starting dose; normal albumin excretion weakens the kidney rationale.
* **Pre-existing conditions:** Severe kidney impairment starts at 20 mg; mild-to-moderate liver impairment is capped at 40 mg ([EMA Micardis EPAR](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/medicines/human/EPAR/micardis)).

  
## Discontinuation & Cycling

* **Long-term use:** Telmisartan is used continuously; its blood pressure effect fades over several days to one week after stopping ([FDA label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=c6eb489d-2946-4c14-839f-d4af761ead31)), so benefits require ongoing use.
* **Withdrawal effects:** No rebound hypertension or withdrawal syndrome is described; blood pressure returns gradually to baseline ([FDA label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=c6eb489d-2946-4c14-839f-d4af761ead31)).
* **Tapering:** No tapering protocol is required; stepping down from 80 to 40 mg before stopping is common practice when other blood pressure drugs need adjusting.
* **Cycling:** No evidence supports cycling, and on-off use would leave blood pressure uncontrolled during off periods.
* **Mandatory stops:** Use stops immediately if pregnancy is detected ([FDA label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=c6eb489d-2946-4c14-839f-d4af761ead31)) and, per guidance for chronic kidney disease, if potassium reaches 6.0 mmol/L ([NICE NG203](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng203/chapter/Recommendations)).

  
## Sourcing and Quality

* **Prescription generics:** Telmisartan is prescription-only; FDA-approved generic tablets (20, 40, 80 mg) from manufacturers such as Glenmark, Alembic, Macleods and Zydus appear in the [DailyMed listing](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/search.cfm?labeltype=all&query=telmisartan), while brand Micardis is discontinued, its 20 and 80 mg strengths not for safety reasons ([Drugs@FDA NDA 020850](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cder/daf/index.cfm?event=overview.process&ApplNo=020850)).
* **Formulations:** Single-drug tablets and fixed combinations with hydrochlorothiazide, amlodipine, or amlodipine plus indapamide exist; single-drug tablets allow independent dose changes.
* **Nitrosamine impurities:** A review traces the 2018 valsartan recalls to NDMA (N-nitrosodimethylamine, a probable carcinogen) formed during its manufacture, and notes that the concern has since extended to other drugs ([Parr 2019](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30458387/)).
* **What to look for:** Regulated pharmacy supply, an intact blister pack and a label matching an approved manufacturer; third-party testing schemes for supplements do not apply to prescription drugs.
* **Unregulated sources:** Bulk telmisartan powder and online "research chemical" products bypass pharmaceutical quality control, so their identity and dose are unverified.

  
## Practical Considerations

* **Time to effect:** Blood pressure falls after the first dose, with most effect within 2 weeks and the maximum by about 4 weeks ([FDA label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=c6eb489d-2946-4c14-839f-d4af761ead31)); albumin and metabolic changes were assessed over months.
* **Common pitfalls:** Combining with an ACE inhibitor, using potassium salt substitutes or regular NSAIDs, skipping follow-up labs, continuing during dehydration, and expecting metabolic or lifespan effects in people without high blood pressure.
* **Regulatory status:** Prescription-only; approved for hypertension and, since 2009, for cardiovascular risk reduction in people unable to take ACE inhibitors ([Drugs@FDA NDA 020850](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cder/daf/index.cfm?event=overview.process&ApplNo=020850)). Use for longevity in people with normal blood pressure is off-label.
* **Cost and access:** Generic telmisartan is widely available and not exceptionally expensive; access mainly requires a prescriber and laboratory monitoring. Because telmisartan and its alternatives are all generic, payers have no strong financial incentive to favor one.

  
## Interaction with Foundational Habits

* **Sleep:** No direct effect on sleep is reported. Indirect: dosing time does not change cardiovascular outcomes ([Mackenzie 2022](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36240838/)), so evening dosing is an option if daytime lightheadedness occurs. Untreated sleep apnea (repeated breathing pauses during sleep) also raises blood pressure.
* **Nutrition:** Potentiating with low-salt eating: high sodium intake blunts drug response, while a DASH-style diet (Dietary Approaches to Stop Hypertension, rich in vegetables, fruit and low-fat dairy) adds to it. Potassium-rich foods are generally safe, but potassium salt substitutes can cause hyperkalemia.
* **Exercise:** Additive blood pressure lowering, so post-exercise lightheadedness and dehydration in heat need attention. Telmisartan did not improve walking in TELEX ([McDermott 2022](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36194220/)), while a small biopsy substudy found larger muscle fibers with exercise plus telmisartan than exercise alone ([Kosmac 2025](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40837832/)).
* **Stress management:** Indirect: stress activates the renin-angiotensin system and raises blood pressure, which telmisartan partly offsets; telmisartan reduced anxiety-like behavior in mice on a high-fat diet ([Huber 2021](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33730932/)). No human cortisol data exist.

  
## Monitoring Protocol & Defining Success

Before starting, baseline testing establishes eligibility and a reference point: home blood pressure averaged over a week, serum potassium, creatinine with eGFR, urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio, HbA1c, liver enzymes, and a pregnancy test where relevant.

Ongoing monitoring follows this cadence: potassium and eGFR at 1–2 weeks after starting and after each dose increase, the schedule advised in chronic kidney disease ([NICE NG203](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng203/chapter/Recommendations)), home blood pressure several times weekly for the first month and then weekly, and potassium, eGFR, urine albumin and HbA1c every 6–12 months once stable. Digoxin or lithium levels are added only when those drugs are taken. Success is defined as home blood pressure at the chosen target without dizziness, stable potassium and kidney function, and, where albuminuria was present, a falling albumin ratio.

| Biomarker | Optimal Functional Range | Why Measure It? | Context/Notes |
|-----------|--------------------------|-----------------|---------------|
| Home blood pressure | Systolic below 120 mm Hg (SPRINT intensive target) | Expected to change; core efficacy marker | Target from [Wright 2015](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26551272/); conventional normal is below 120/80 mm Hg; seated after 5 minutes' rest, morning and evening |
| Serum potassium | 3.5–5.0 mmol/L (standard reference range) | Safety check; high values change or stop use | In chronic kidney disease, not started above 5.0 and stopped at 6.0 or more ([NICE NG203](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng203/chapter/Recommendations)); hemolyzed (damaged) samples falsely raise values |
| Creatinine and eGFR | Creatinine about 0.6–1.3 mg/dL (standard reference range); eGFR: No established target, track change from own baseline | Safety check; large falls change use | An eGFR below 60 mL/min/1.73 m² is a chronic kidney disease cutoff, not a reference range; review if creatinine rises 30% or eGFR falls 25% (threshold for chronic kidney disease, [NICE NG203](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng203/chapter/Recommendations)); a small early dip is expected; heavy exercise in the prior 24 hours raises creatinine |

| HbA1c | 4.0–5.6% (standard reference range) | Expected to change modestly in insulin resistance | No fasting needed; pair with fasting glucose |

Qualitative markers:

* Lightheadedness or dizziness on standing, especially in the morning or after exercise
* Energy levels and fatigue during the first weeks
* Exercise tolerance and recovery
* Muscle pain or weakness, for which creatine kinase (a muscle enzyme in blood) is the usual test
* Any swelling of the lips, tongue or face, a medical emergency

  
## Emerging Research

* **Parkinson's disease platform trial:** The phase 3 EJS ACT-PD trial ([NCT07207057](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07207057)) compares telmisartan and terazosin with a shared placebo arm, enrolling up to 1,200 participants across all arms, measuring progression on a standard Parkinson's rating scale through 2031. A positive result would establish a first neuroprotective signal; a null result would weaken brain-protection claims.
* **Alzheimer's disease trial status:** SARTAN-AD ([NCT02085265](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02085265)), comparing telmisartan with perindopril on brain atrophy, was terminated in February 2025 after enrolling 23 participants; no results are posted. The observational signal in African Americans ([Zhang 2023](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36331056/)) remains untested in a randomized trial.
* **Prostate cancer tolerability trial:** A phase 1 trial ([NCT06168487](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06168487)) gives telmisartan alone or with standard therapy to 36 men, with tolerability as the primary endpoint, through 2027. Good tolerability would support safety in people with cancer; poor tolerability would add caution to use alongside cancer care.
* **Ovarian cancer trial:** A phase 2 single-arm trial ([NCT06815497](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06815497)) adds telmisartan to chemotherapy in 33 women whose cancer progressed despite platinum chemotherapy, measuring time without cancer growth through 2029. A positive result would counter the cancer-risk concern; a null result would leave anticancer claims unsupported.
* **Low-dose triple combinations:** TRIDENT, completed and published, cut recurrent stroke with a telmisartan 20 mg triple tablet (HR 0.61; 95% CI 0.41–0.92) ([Anderson 2026](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42019018/)), cited under cardiovascular benefits; further trials could clarify telmisartan's own contribution.
* **Lifespan testing:** The Interventions Testing Program found no lifespan gain for atorvastatin plus telmisartan in mice ([Korstanje 2026](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41843349/)); telmisartan alone has not been tested, a gap that could strengthen or weaken the case for slowing aging.
* **Kidney safety in healthy kidneys:** The post hoc signal of excess kidney events without albuminuria ([Tobe 2011](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21357827/)) has not been tested prospectively and bears directly on use by people with normal kidneys.
* **Muscle and exercise:** Small biopsy findings of larger muscle fibers with exercise ([Kosmac 2025](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40837832/)) need confirmation in trials measuring strength or function in healthy older adults.

  
## Conclusion

Telmisartan is a long-acting blood pressure medication that blocks a vessel-narrowing hormone and, in laboratory work, partly switches on a cellular regulator of sugar and fat handling. Its best-supported effects, shown by independent groups, are lower blood pressure and less protein leakage into urine. In people at high risk of heart disease it prevented heart attack, stroke and related death about as well as a standard alternative, with a modest, uncertain margin over a dummy treatment.

Beyond blood pressure, the picture is mixed. Small blood sugar gains appear mainly in people with high blood pressure and a weak response to insulin, and randomized trials showed no protection of memory, walking in narrowed leg arteries, artery bulges or fatty liver. Evidence for slower aging rests on animal work, and the one formal mouse lifespan test, using a combination with a cholesterol drug, was negative.

The main risks are dizziness or fainting from low blood pressure, high potassium, reduced kidney filtering, which may matter most for people with healthy kidneys, and serious harm to an unborn child. An early cancer signal was not borne out for this drug class used alone.

The evidence pairs large, well-run heart trials with small, often conflicting studies for other claims, and the large trials were largely funded by the original manufacturer. For health-focused adults with normal blood pressure, the case for telmisartan as a longevity tool remains uncertain; for those with raised blood pressure or kidney protein leakage, its benefits are better established.
